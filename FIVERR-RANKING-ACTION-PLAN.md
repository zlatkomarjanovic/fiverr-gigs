## What Not To Do

- Do not buy fake clicks.
- Do not run bots through the gig URLs.
- Do not favorite gigs from fake accounts.
- Do not spam the same link everywhere.
- Do not keep changing titles daily.

Those can suppress the account. Real ranking comes from query match, CTR, messages, orders, response time, and completion quality.
